# Great Dictator study clip

`dictator-3-study.mp4` is the audiovisual study clip supplied by the repository
owner, derived from the final speech in *The Great Dictator* (1940), written
and directed by Charles Chaplin. The source study copy starts at the original
`Dictator 3.m4v` file's 90-second point.
