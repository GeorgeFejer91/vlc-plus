VLC+ and Remote LSL Recorder

Installs two separate programs for your Windows account and adds a desktop
shortcut that opens both. Individual program entries are also available in
the Start menu. Each program keeps its own settings and uninstaller.

The launcher does not start a recording, enable phone access, or start an
experiment. Select streams and start recording in Remote LSL Recorder, then
play your video in VLC+. The Great Dictator study clip is in VLC+'s
examples\media folder, with its separate media permission notice.

Stop/save any recording and close VLC+ and Remote LSL Recorder before
installing this bundle. The installer refuses while either app is open.

Uninstalling the joint launcher removes its shortcuts. The two applications
remain installed; uninstall them separately through Windows when desired.
Recordings are never removed by the joint launcher.

All three installer choices are available in the GitHub release: this joint
installer first, VLC+ alone, and Remote LSL Recorder alone.
